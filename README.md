# Data-Analysis-Portfolio
In this file, I analyzed a sales performance dataset for a tech/retail business that specializes in electronics, evaluating the financial progress, monthly revenue trends, performance by region, and sales channel distribution. As well as getting more insights on what products are proffered by the customers.

I asked myself, after taking a good look at the type of data I have in my possession, “What are the important questions that need answers?”

And they were:

- What is the revenue produced by the company and quantity sold? 

<img width="693" height="62" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dbd31e75-c5a3-4b8f-ada3-5f01dc267d43" />

The business produced a total amount of 2.45 M in the last 12 months, with over 2000 orders placed.


- What are some important key insights, such as the monthly trend? 
November ($274K), June ($259K), and April ($241K) bring the highest revenue, while the weakest point is reached in July ($151K) and May ($154K).

- How well is every region performing? 
The primary revenue drivers are the North ($562K) and Central ($557K) regions while the West ($421K) region is on the last place.

The Online sales channel produces the most revenue, making it the dominant channel with a high of $792K, followed closely by the Corporate Sales with $719K. 



- What are the products that perform well? 
The top 3 products that have sold the best within this time period are Gaming Laptops ($433K), Laptop Air 13($274K) and Laptop Pro 14 ($263K).

Business Recommendations: 

- Prepare inventory for the peak revenue months (June, April, November). 

- Allocate Marketing budget for the slow periods (May & July), run campaigns or create offers/bundles to control cash flow.

- Conduct a market analysis for the West region to determine if the cause of the low engagement and revenue is due to lower brand awareness, logistics issues, or something else.

- Prioritize supply chain availability for the highest products, such as the Gaming Laptops.

- Increase and add diversity in the product promotions to reduce the revenue dependency that comes from the Gaming Laptops and even the revenue gaps between the top 5 products and the rest. 



The analysis and the project overall took me from my comfort zone, where I analyze only what I am told at my current job, to a new zone where I have the control, as I own the business and I need those answers myself. The task helped shape my mind and train my eye better to spot what information is relevant and my critical thinking to understand why it would be relevant, as well as coming up with solutions and new perspectives I would want to obtain as a business owner.
